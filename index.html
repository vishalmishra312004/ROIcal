A USEFUL RADIO SCORECARD / 03

Pick the KPI that fits the brief.

Radio can play different jobs. Agree on the outcome before the first spot airs.

A / AWARENESS

Did people notice?

Net reach, average frequency, target rating points, ad recall and brand awareness.

Study: pre / post brand survey · audience source
B / CONSIDERATION

Did interest rise?

Branded search, direct traffic, campaign page visits, app opens and store locator use.

Study: search trends · tagged landing page
C / RESPONSE

Did someone act?

Calls, WhatsApp starts, QR scans, enquiries, qualified leads, sign-ups and cost per lead.

Study: unique number / URL · CRM source
D / RETAIL & DEALER

Did footfall convert?

Dealer visits, store footfall, test drives, coupon use, POS sales and cost per visit.

Study: matched outlets · POS / dealer data

HOW TO READ THIS MODEL / 04

Delivery is one half of the proof.

Airtime and audience estimates show what the campaign delivered. Response data shows what happened alongside it. An attribution design helps estimate what the campaign caused.

01

Gross impressions
Spots aired × average audience per spot

02

Estimated frequency
Gross impressions ÷ estimated net reach

03

GRPs
Gross impressions ÷ target audience × 100

04

Modeled ROI
(Attributed sales × margin − campaign spend) ÷ spend

Measurement note. Audience, reach and response inputs should come from the same market and campaign window. Radio industry definitions for reach, frequency and gross impressions are summarized by the Radio Advertising Bureau. This prototype applies no external ROI benchmark.

For an actual case study, report the source, period, sample, comparison method and any other media or promotions running at the same time.
